# Gedi the Tarzan — Aana Swing Saga

> A browser-based 3D swing-runner. Thrissur's biggest elephant fan swings from trunk to tail across Kerala to recover the stolen Golden Nettipattam before the Pooram fireworks.

## One-liner
**Hold to grab an elephant's trunk or tail, release to fly.** Chain nose → tail → next nose, never touch the ground, and chase a banana-addicted monkey across five Kerala chapters.

## The hero
**Adarshh S Kumar — "Gedi"** (Thrissur slang for *buddy*). Knows every tusker's name, height and horoscope. Wears a wristwatch even while flying. Rendered as a chibi bobblehead with his real face (from the photos in `Adarshh Images/`), a "GEDI" tee and jeans.

## Story — *"Operation Nettipattam"*
It's Thrissur Pooram morning. 30 decorated tuskers, a million people, chenda drums warming up.
Then disaster: **Kurangan Kuttappan**, Kerala's most notorious monkey, snatches the **Golden Nettipattam** off the forehead of the legendary tusker **Gajaveeran Ambadi Kannan**. No Nettipattam, no Pooram.
The elephants raise their trunks. Only one man speaks fluent Elephant. *"Gedi, pidicho!"* (Grab on!)

| # | Chapter | Setting | New twist |
|---|---------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | Pooram Morning | Vadakkunnathan temple grounds, golden elephants, parasols | Learn swinging; parasol bounces; chenda beat bonus |
| 2 | Athirappilly Chase | Jungle + waterfall | Monkeys throw bananas, coconuts drop |
| 3 | Backwater Bedlam | Alappuzha backwaters | Ground is water; snake boats & houseboat roofs bounce you |
| 4 | Munnar Mist | Tea hills, fog, cold | Sleepy elephants (droopy trunks), sneezy elephants (launch) |
| 5 | Pooram Night | Fireworks finale | Everything at once; catch Kuttappan, win the Nettipattam |
| ∞ | Encore | All biomes cycling | Endless, faster — pure high-score chase |

Each chapter ends with Kuttappan taunting Gedi from a palm tree. Swing into him to make him drop a clue and flee to the next region. In chapter 5 Gedi snatches the Nettipattam back → fireworks → victory dance (the real dance photo) → Encore mode.

## Quirks & fun
- **Stretchy trunks & tails** — elephants physically stretch their trunk/tail to catch Gedi.
- **Bobblehead Gedi** with his real face, flailing limbs when airborne, dizzy stars when bonked.
- **Manglish hype callouts:** *PWOLI! · KIDU! · ADIPOLI! · THAKARTHU! · MASS! · ISHTAA!*
- **Chenda beat bonus ("Thaalam!")** — release on the drum beat for a boost.
- **Sneezy elephant** — "AACHOO!" blasts Gedi sky-high.
- **KSRTC bus rescue** — fall onto the red bus roof and bounce back to safety.
- **Chaya power** — a glass of hot tea gives one free save. **Pazhampori** fritters are coins. **Chakka** (jackfruit) = extra life.
- **Kuttiyaana** baby elephants — tiny trunks, double points.
- Optional announcer voice (browser speech) shouting the callouts.

## Platform
Runs in any modern browser (desktop + mobile), detects device type/OS/browser to tune controls and graphics, keeps a per-device high score, and has an optional global leaderboard when deployed on Vercel.
